The generic type parameters of `ArraySpec` and `GroupSpec` (v2 and v3) now carry defaults (PEP 696), so the bare class names are fully usable without parametrization, while `ArraySpec[MyAttrs]` and subclass-level field narrowing keep working. `BaseGroupSpec` — a model of a group's own metadata, without members — is promoted from the experimental module to `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3`: `to_flat` now emits it for every group (breaking: flat groups were previously `GroupSpec` instances with `members=None`), `from_flat` accepts it alongside the existing forms, and group `like()` comparisons against a `BaseGroupSpec` compare the group's own metadata. The `with_*` builder methods (`with_shape`, `with_data_type`, `with_fill_value`, and the rest, plus `with_members` and `with_attributes`) are likewise promoted from the experimental module; every builder returns a revalidated copy. New `TypeIs`-based narrowing helpers (`is_array_spec`, `is_group_spec`, `is_base_group_spec` in both format modules) tell flattened-hierarchy values apart with full static narrowing, and the `GroupSpec` methods that override `BaseGroupSpec` are marked `@override`.
