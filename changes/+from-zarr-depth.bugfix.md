Restored the `depth` parameter of `GroupSpec.from_zarr` and the module-level `from_zarr`, in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3`, which 0.11.0 removed without a deprecation period, breaking code written for 0.10 such as every read in `ome-zarr-models`. `depth` is deprecated along with the rest of the zarr-python IO and goes in 0.12. `depth=0` reads the group without its members and returns a `BaseGroupSpec`, parametrized with the calling class's attributes type, since a `GroupSpec` always holds its members. Any other depth reads the whole hierarchy, since a `GroupSpec` cannot hold groups whose members were not read; use `from_zarr_flat(node, depth=n)` to read part of a hierarchy. A depth lower than -1 raises `ValueError`, as in 0.10.
