The JSON schemas of specs that hold extra fields (v2 and v3 `ArraySpec`, v3 `BaseGroupSpec` and `GroupSpec`, and models that nest them) describe the documents they read and write, in both `model_json_schema` modes: they no longer list an `extra_fields` property and refuse every other member, but admit undeclared top-level members typed as JSON values, and refuse a member named `extra_fields`.
