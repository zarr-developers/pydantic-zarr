Removed six names from `pydantic_zarr.v3` that were kept as aliases of `zarr-metadata` types since 0.11: `RegularChunkingConfig`, `RegularChunking`, `DefaultChunkKeyEncodingConfig`, `DefaultChunkKeyEncoding`, `V2ChunkKeyEncodingConfig` and `V2ChunkKeyEncoding`. Import the types they named from `zarr_metadata.v3.chunk_grid.regular` (`RegularChunkGridConfiguration`, `RegularChunkGridMetadata`), `zarr_metadata.v3.chunk_key_encoding.default` (`DefaultChunkKeyEncodingConfiguration`, `DefaultChunkKeyEncodingObject`) and `zarr_metadata.v3.chunk_key_encoding.v2` (`V2ChunkKeyEncodingConfiguration`, `V2ChunkKeyEncodingObject`).
