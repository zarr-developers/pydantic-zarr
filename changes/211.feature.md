`GroupSpec` (v2 and v3) takes the type of its whole `members` mapping as its second type parameter, `TMembers`. A closed `TypedDict` fixes a group's member names in one parameterization, `GroupSpec[Attrs, Members]`, where a subclass that overrode the `members` field was needed before; a `Mapping` constrains the type of every member. The default of `members`, an empty mapping, is validated against the members type, so a group whose members type requires a member cannot leave `members` out.
