`model_json_schema(mode="serialization")` on a spec or an `AttributesBase` model lists the model's fields again, as the validation-mode schema does, where it returned an open object (`{"additionalProperties": true, "type": "object"}`). The `TypedDict`s of `replace`'s keyword arguments (`ArraySpecUpdate`, `BaseGroupSpecUpdate` and `GroupSpecUpdate`, v2 and v3) are closed, which makes `GroupSpec.replace` a type-safe override of `BaseGroupSpec.replace`.
