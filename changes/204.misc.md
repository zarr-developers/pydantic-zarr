The test suite draws Zarr V3 array and group documents from `zarr-metadata`'s Hypothesis strategies (`zarr_metadata.testing.strategies`, in the release after 0.6.0 behind its `testing` extra) and checks that `ArraySpec` and `BaseGroupSpec` read everything `zarr-metadata` draws as valid and write back what it reads without a problem. The places where the two still disagree are recorded as strict expected failures. The tests are skipped where the strategies are not installed.
