The test suite draws Zarr V3 array and group documents from `zarr-metadata`'s Hypothesis strategies (`zarr_metadata.testing.strategies`, in the release after 0.6.0 behind its `testing` extra), in every envelope spelling and from every definition they hold, and checks that `ArraySpec` and `BaseGroupSpec` read everything `zarr-metadata` draws as valid, as the spec its canonical spelling gives, and write back what `zarr-metadata` reads without a problem. The two places where the readers still disagree with `zarr-metadata` are recorded as strict expected failures. The tests are skipped where the strategies are not installed.
