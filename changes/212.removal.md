The `pydantic_zarr.experimental` module is deprecated and will be removed in pydantic-zarr 0.13. Importing it, or any of its submodules, now emits a `DeprecationWarning`. Use `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3` instead: they define `BaseGroupSpec`, and a subclass of their `ArraySpec` or `GroupSpec` can annotate `attributes` and `members` directly, as the experimental classes do.
