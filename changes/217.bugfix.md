Fixed attribute models being dumped under their Python field names when a spec was serialized other than through its own `model_dump` or `model_dump_json`: through a `TypeAdapter`, as a field of another pydantic model, or with `by_alias=None`. Attribute aliases (such as `_FillValue` or `image-label`) now apply on every serialization path unless the dump passes `by_alias=False`, including to models held in an attributes mapping.
