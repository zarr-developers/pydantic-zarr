`GroupSpec.members` in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3` is no longer nullable: it is always a mapping, and still defaults to `{}`, so an empty mapping always means "no members". A group whose members are unknown is now a `BaseGroupSpec`, matching the experimental modules. `GroupSpec.from_zarr(group, depth=n)` reads each subgroup at the depth limit as a `BaseGroupSpec` member, instead of a `GroupSpec` with `members=None`, and refuses `depth=0`, since that reads no members. The module-level `from_zarr(group, depth=0)` returns a `BaseGroupSpec`. A member document without a `members` key now validates as a `BaseGroupSpec`, so such members survive `model_dump` and validation. `GroupSpec.like` no longer treats `members=None` as a separate state, and `GroupSpec.to_zarr` writes a `BaseGroupSpec` member as a group with no members. To migrate, replace `GroupSpec(members=None, ...)` with `BaseGroupSpec(...)`, including in the flat mappings passed to `from_flat`. Replace `GroupSpec.from_zarr(group, depth=0)` with `BaseGroupSpec.from_zarr(group)`, and replace `members is None` checks with `isinstance(node, GroupSpec)`.
