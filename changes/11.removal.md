`GroupSpec.members` in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3` is no longer nullable, and a `GroupSpec` is now always a complete hierarchy. `members` is a mapping of `ArraySpec` and `GroupSpec` values that still defaults to `{}`, so an empty mapping always means "no members". `BaseGroupSpec` is no longer accepted as a member. A group whose members are unknown is a `BaseGroupSpec`, which only appears on its own or in a flat hierarchy. `GroupSpec.from_zarr` and the module-level `from_zarr` lose their `depth` parameter and always read the whole hierarchy. The new `from_zarr_flat(node, depth=n)` reads part of a hierarchy into the flat form that `to_flat` produces and `from_flat` takes. To migrate, replace `GroupSpec(members=None, ...)` with `BaseGroupSpec(...)`, including in the flat mappings passed to `from_flat`. Replace `GroupSpec.from_zarr(group, depth=0)` with `BaseGroupSpec.from_zarr(group)`, and other `depth` reads with `from_zarr_flat(group, depth=n)`.
