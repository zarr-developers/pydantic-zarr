Switched type checking from mypy to pyrefly. The entry points are unchanged (`hatch run types:check`, `just typecheck`, and the `typecheck` CI job), and the configuration lives in `[tool.pyrefly]` in `pyproject.toml`. The error categories corresponding to the mypy settings this project had disabled (`disallow_any_generics`, `warn_return_any`) stay disabled for now, with a TODO to enable them. Along the way, the `type(self)(**{...})` reconstruction idiom was rewritten as `type(self).model_validate({...})` (same validation semantics, properly typed), and `pydantic_zarr.v2.ArraySpec.from_array` now converts `numcodecs.abc.Codec` compressors and filters to their dict form before constructing the spec.
