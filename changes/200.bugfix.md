Stopped `AttributesBase` and the experimental v3 `ArraySpec` and `GroupSpec` from passing an `extra` setting to the TypedDicts they hold. Building a model whose attributes nest a closed TypedDict no longer emits pydantic's `TypedDictExtraConfigWarning`, and each nested TypedDict now keeps or drops unknown keys as it would under any other model. `AttributesBase` keeps the keys it does not declare apart from its fields, read through a read-only `extra_fields` mapping: a frozen attributes model hashes by its declared fields, an attribute named `extra_fields` round-trips like any other key, and a subclass that sets `extra="allow"` is refused.
