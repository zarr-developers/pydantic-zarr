The entry points that read or write zarr-python nodes are deprecated and will be removed in pydantic-zarr 0.12: `to_zarr` and `from_zarr` on `ArraySpec`, `BaseGroupSpec` and `GroupSpec`, the module-level `to_zarr`, `from_zarr` and `from_zarr_flat`, `like` given a `zarr.Array` or `zarr.Group`, and `pydantic_zarr.core.walk_zarr_hierarchy`, in both `v2` and `v3`, and v3's `ArraySpec.from_array` given a `zarr.Array` (v2's `from_array` reads any array-like by its attributes, and a `zarr.Array` is one). pydantic-zarr models metadata documents; reading them from a store and writing them to one is zarr-python's job. Each entry point now warns once, with a `DeprecationWarning` that names its replacement: write the documents `to_stored_metadata_json` gives with zarr-python (`zarr.create_hierarchy`), and read a node's stored documents with zarr-python into `from_stored_metadata_json`. The usage guides show both, and the example scripts use them.
