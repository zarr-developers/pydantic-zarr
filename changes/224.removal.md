Removed the zarr-python IO entry points deprecated in 0.11, in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3`: `to_zarr` and `from_zarr` on `ArraySpec`, `BaseGroupSpec` and `GroupSpec`, the module-level `to_zarr`, `from_zarr` and `from_zarr_flat`, `like` given a `zarr.Array` or `zarr.Group`, v3's `ArraySpec.from_array` given a `zarr.Array`, and `pydantic_zarr.core.walk_zarr_hierarchy`. pydantic-zarr models metadata documents and leaves reading and writing them to zarr-python: write the documents `to_stored_metadata_json` gives with zarr-python (for instance through `zarr.create_hierarchy`), and pass the documents zarr-python stored to `from_stored_metadata_json`. The usage guide's "Reading and writing with zarr-python" section has both helpers. `pydantic_zarr.experimental` keeps its own IO until it is removed in 0.13.
