Fixed the `@overload` signatures of `pydantic_zarr.v2.from_zarr` and `pydantic_zarr.experimental.v2.from_zarr`, which declared `depth` without a default and so rejected the documented single-argument call `from_zarr(group)` under a type checker.

Widened `pydantic_zarr.experimental.v2.GroupSpec.from_flat` to accept `Mapping[str, ArraySpec | BaseGroupSpec]`, matching what `GroupSpec.to_flat` returns and what the module-level `from_flat` already accepted. Round-tripping `GroupSpec.from_flat(group.to_flat())` previously failed to type-check.
