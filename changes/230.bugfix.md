A v2 or v3 `GroupSpec` whose members type is a closed `TypedDict` keeps its members in the order they were given, where it held them, and wrote their documents, in the order the `TypedDict` declares them. A `TypedDict` with `extra_items`, used as the members or the attributes type of a spec, no longer needs `@with_config(ConfigDict())` to keep pydantic from warning that the spec's `extra="forbid"` setting does not apply to it.
