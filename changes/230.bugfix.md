Members of a v2 or v3 `GroupSpec` typed with a closed `TypedDict` now stay in the order they were given. Previously, they were reordered to match the `TypedDict`'s declaration order. Using a `TypedDict` with `extra_items` as a spec's members or attributes type no longer triggers a pydantic warning, so the `@with_config(ConfigDict())` workaround is no longer needed.
